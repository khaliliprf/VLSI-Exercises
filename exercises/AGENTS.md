# Exercise solution instructions

Apply the project-root `AGENTS.md` together with these exercise-specific rules.

## Problem interpretation and derivation
- Read the original problem statement and inspect referenced circuit figures before solving. Use the relevant lecture slides and designated textbook sections, and identify conflicts in notation or assumptions.
- State known values, unknowns, assumptions, boundary conditions, and the model being used before deriving the answer.
- Assume the learner has not passed Digital Electronics and is not confident in mathematics. Explain necessary logic and circuit prerequisites, define symbols, and show nontrivial algebra step by step.
- Explain why the selected equation and method apply. Check units, signs, operating regions, physical roots, supply limits, boundary cases, and continuity where relevant.
- Distinguish exact identities, approximations, model predictions, and measurements. Do not imply SPICE simulation or experimental verification unless performed.
- Introduce important displayed equations in prose. Move dense formula chains out of inline text. Put a governing expression on the left and its operating conditions on the right when that arrangement clarifies the solution.

## Sources and maintained outputs
- Preserve the original exercise numbering in Persian digits in Persian prose and headings. Keep dotted exercise identifiers readable and directionally correct.
- Treat Markdown drafts, finalized LaTeX solutions, review reports, and archived PDFs according to their documented status. Do not regenerate reviewed LaTeX mechanically from Markdown when that would discard corrections.
- Keep each series in its own `exercises/N/` folder and assemble the submission from that series' source fragments. Preserve established cover metadata and designated final PDF names.
- Cite the source title and section or page, distinguishing printed pages from PDF page indices. Inspect diagrams directly when extracted text does not establish circuit connectivity.
- After a change, build the affected assignment and inspect its rendered PDF, references, Persian digits, formula direction, and page layout. Keep output verification evidence accurate.
